Good questions

Can you handle Saturday rental turnovers on Anna Maria Island? Yes. We schedule turnovers around your check-out and check-in times, including busy Saturday changeovers.

What is included in a standard house cleaning? A standard Spiffinity house cleaning covers kitchens, bathrooms, bedrooms, living areas, hallways and entryways: dusting, HEPA vacuuming, mopping, sanitizing high-touch surfaces, cleaning mirrors and glass, and taking out the trash. Oven, fridge, cabinet interiors and laundry are available as add-ons.

How much does house cleaning cost in Bradenton? Pricing depends on your home’s size, number of bathrooms, current condition, how often you book and any add-ons. Recurring visits usually cost less per clean than one-time service. Spiffinity gives free, no-obligation quotes; request one online or call (941) 993-9614.

Do I need to be home during the cleaning? No. Many clients leave secure entry instructions or a keyless code. Our fully insured, background-checked team locks up exactly as instructed.
