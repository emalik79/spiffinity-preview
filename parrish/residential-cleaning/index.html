House Cleaning in Parrish

Straight answers about our services, products, pricing and scheduling.

Are your products safe for kids and pets in Parrish homes?

Yes. We use non-toxic, Green Seal and EPA Safer Choice certified products that are safe for children and pets.

What is included in a standard house cleaning?

A standard Spiffinity house cleaning covers kitchens, bathrooms, bedrooms, living areas, hallways and entryways: dusting, HEPA vacuuming, mopping, sanitizing high-touch surfaces, cleaning mirrors and glass, and taking out the trash. Oven, fridge, cabinet interiors and laundry are available as add-ons.

How much does house cleaning cost in Bradenton?

Pricing depends on your home’s size, number of bathrooms, current condition, how often you book and any add-ons. Recurring visits usually cost less per clean than one-time service. Spiffinity gives free, no-obligation quotes; request one online or call (941) 993-9614.

Do I need to be home during the cleaning?

No. Many clients leave secure entry instructions or a keyless code. Our fully insured, background-checked team locks up exactly as instructed.

Can I book recurring cleanings?

Yes. Choose weekly, bi-weekly or monthly visits. Recurring clients also get a rotating deep-clean system so every part of the home gets detailed attention over time.
